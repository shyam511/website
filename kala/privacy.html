This Privacy Policy explains how the Kala Android application (the “app”) handles information. It applies to the app and to this page. By using the app you agree to this policy.

Overview

Kala is a voice-first image creator. You sign in with Google, then describe an image — or point at part of one and say what to change — and a cloud AI model generates it. To do that, your prompts, the audio you speak during a session, and any photo you choose to import are sent to Google’s Gemini models through Firebase AI Logic. Your account, your projects and how you use the app are handled through Google Firebase. We do not run our own servers and we do not sell your information.

1. Information we process

To create images and run your account, the app processes the following.

  • Account details — when you sign in with Google, we receive your name, email address, profile photo and a user identifier. This is handled by Firebase Authentication.
  • Prompts — the words you say or type to describe an image or a change to one.
  • Voice audio — while a session is active, your microphone is used to hold a live conversation with the model. The audio is streamed to our AI provider, used for the current request and not stored by us.
  • Images — photos you choose to import, and the images the model generates from your prompts.
  • Projects — the conversation and the images it produced, saved on your device and synced to your account’s cloud storage.
  • Usage and analytics — how you use the app, an app-instance and device identifier, your approximate region, and diagnostic and crash information, collected by Google Analytics for Firebase.
  • Subscription status — handled by Google Play. We do not receive or store your payment details.

2. How information is used

Your information is used only to operate the features you use:

  • to sign you in and keep your account secure, and to prevent misuse of the paid AI models;
  • to send your speech to text and to generate or change an image with the AI model;
  • to save your project on your device and sync it to your account so you can reopen it, or open it on another device;
  • to understand how the app is used and to improve it, using analytics.

3. Sharing and third parties

We do not sell or rent your information, and there are no advertising or data-broker SDKs in the app. A few third parties are involved, all part of Google’s Firebase and Gemini services:

  • Google — Firebase AI Logic. Your prompts, images and audio are sent to Google’s Gemini models through Firebase AI Logic, which keeps the provider key server-side and protects the models with Firebase App Check. Google processes this data as the model provider under Google’s Privacy Policy at policies.google.com/privacy.
  • Google — Firebase Authentication and Cloud storage. Your account details and your synced projects are stored using Firebase Authentication and Firebase cloud storage (Cloud Firestore and Cloud Storage), under Google’s Privacy Policy.
  • Google — Analytics for Firebase. Usage, device and diagnostic information is collected by Google Analytics for Firebase to help us understand how the app is used. You can learn how Google uses this data at policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites.
  • Google Play. If you buy a subscription, Google Play handles the transaction under Google’s own terms and privacy policy. We do not receive your payment details.

4. Information stored on your device and in your account

  • On your device — your projects and your app settings. This lets the app work offline and reopen your work.
  • In your account — your account details and your synced projects, held in Google Firebase so they follow you to a new device.

You can delete a project in the app, and you can delete your whole account and everything in it as described below.

5. Microphone, photos and permissions

  • Account. Sign in with Google is required before you can make images, so that every request is tied to a real account.
  • Microphone (RECORD_AUDIO). Used only while an editing session is active. A clear indicator is shown whenever the app is listening, and the microphone sleeps when the canvas is idle or the app is in the background. The app does not store your audio after the request.
  • Photos. You choose images through the Android photo picker; the app accesses only the images you pick.
  • Internet. Used to reach Firebase AI Logic and to sync your account.

6. AI-generated content

Images are generated by an AI model and may be inaccurate or unexpected. Images generated by Google’s Gemini model carry Google’s invisible SynthID watermark. The app filters prompts and results for unsafe content, blocks attempts to alter real people’s faces, reproduce brand logos or create sexual content, and provides an in-app way to report offensive output without leaving the app.

7. Data retention and deletion

Your account details and synced projects are kept while your account exists. You can delete your account at any time:

  • In the app: open Settings, tap Delete account, and confirm.
  • On the web: visit bitlane.in/kala/delete-account.

Deleting your account erases your account details and your synced projects. Projects stored on your device are removed when you delete them in the app or uninstall the app. How long our AI provider retains prompts, images and audio processed through its services is governed by Google’s terms and settings.

8. Data security

Information is transmitted over HTTPS (voice uses an encrypted connection). Your account data and synced projects are held in Google Firebase under Google’s security controls. As with any software, no method of transmission or storage is perfectly secure.

9. Children’s privacy

Kala is not directed to children, and we do not knowingly collect information from children.

10. Your privacy rights

Privacy laws such as the EU/UK General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR), the California Consumer Privacy Act (CCPA/CPRA) and India’s Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023 (DPDP Act) give individuals rights over their personal data, including access, correction, deletion and objection. Because you have an account, you can exercise the deletion right yourself using Delete account in the app or the web page above. For any other request, contact us at the address below.

11. Changes to this policy

If we change this policy, we will update the “Last updated” date above and publish the new version at the same URL. Material changes will be reflected in a new release of the app where required.
