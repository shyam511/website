This Privacy Policy explains how the Kala Android application (the “app”) handles information. It applies to the app and to this page. By using the app you agree to this policy.

Overview

Kala is a voice-first image creator. You describe an image — or point at part of one and say what to change — and a cloud AI model generates it. To do that, your prompts, the audio you speak during a session, and any photo you choose to import are sent to our AI provider, Google, through Firebase AI Logic. We do not run our own servers and we do not have accounts. Everything you make is stored on your device.

1. Information we process

To create images, the app processes the following. This information leaves your device only to reach our AI provider for generation, as described below.

  • Prompts — the words you say or type to describe an image or a change to one.
  • Voice audio — while a session is active, your microphone is used to turn your speech into words. The audio is used for the current request and is not stored by us.
  • Images — photos you choose to import, and the images the model generates from your prompts.
  • Subscription status — handled by Google Play. We do not receive or store your payment details.

We do not collect your name, email address, contacts, location, device identifiers or advertising ID. The app has no account system.

2. How information is used

Your prompts, voice audio and images are used only to operate the features you use:

  • sent to our AI provider to turn your speech into text and to generate or change an image;
  • kept on your device as a project (the conversation and the images it produced) so you can reopen it and keep working.

3. Sharing and third parties

We do not sell or rent your information. There are no advertising, analytics or data-broker SDKs in the app. Two third parties are involved:

  • Google — Gemini image model and Firebase AI Logic. Google processes your prompts, images and audio as our service provider in order to generate images and transcribe speech. Google’s handling is described in Google’s Privacy Policy at policies.google.com/privacy and the Firebase data-processing terms.
  • Google Play. If you buy a subscription, Google Play handles the transaction under Google’s own terms and privacy policy. We do not receive your payment details.

4. Information stored on your device

The app saves the following locally so it can work. It stays on your device and is not sent to us:

  • Projects — the conversation and the images it produced.
  • Settings — your app preferences.

You can remove this at any time by deleting a project in the app or by uninstalling the app. Because we hold no accounts and no servers of our own, there is no deletion request to send us.

5. Microphone, photos and permissions

  • Microphone (RECORD_AUDIO). Used only while an editing session is active. A clear indicator is shown whenever the app is listening, and the microphone sleeps when the canvas is idle or the app is in the background. Audio is not retained after the request.
  • Photos. You choose images through the Android photo picker; the app accesses only the images you pick.
  • Internet. Used to reach the AI provider.

6. AI-generated content

Images are generated by an AI model and may be inaccurate or unexpected. Images generated by Google’s Gemini model carry Google’s invisible SynthID watermark. The app filters prompts and results for unsafe content, blocks attempts to alter real people’s faces, reproduce brand logos or create sexual content, and provides an in-app way to report offensive output without leaving the app.

7. Data retention and deletion

We retain nothing on our side — we have no servers storing your information. Data the app stores remains on your device until you delete it or uninstall the app. How long Google retains prompts, images and audio processed through its API is governed by Google’s terms and settings.

8. Data security

Information sent to the AI provider is transmitted over HTTPS. Information the app stores stays in the app’s private storage on your device. As with any software, no method of transmission or storage is perfectly secure.

9. Children’s privacy

Kala is not directed to children, and we do not knowingly collect information from children.

10. Your privacy rights

Privacy laws such as the EU/UK General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR), the California Consumer Privacy Act (CCPA/CPRA) and India’s Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023 (DPDP Act) give individuals rights over their personal data, including access, correction, deletion and objection. We hold no accounts and no personal data about you; the information processed to create your images is transient and handled by our AI provider. If you have a question about this policy or believe we hold something about you, contact us at the address below.

11. Changes to this policy

If we change this policy, we will update the “Last updated” date above and publish the new version at the same URL. Material changes will be reflected in a new release of the app where required.
