Kala · Account

Delete your account

Kala signs you in with Google and keeps your projects with your account. You can delete your account and everything in it at any time. This cannot be undone.

Delete it in the app

  1. Open Kala and go to Settings.
  2. Under Account, tap Delete account.
  3. Confirm. Your account and your synced projects are erased.

Request deletion here

If you cannot open the app, email us from the Google address you signed in with, and ask us to delete your Kala account. We will confirm within 7 days.

Email: healthcraftio@gmail.com

What is deleted

  • Your account details (name, email address, profile photo and user id).
  • Every project synced to your account, including the conversation and the generated images.
  • Your remaining free allowance and, if you subscribed, the link between your account and the subscription.

Projects still stored on a device are removed when you delete them in the app or uninstall the app. An active Google Play subscription is managed by Google Play; deleting your Kala account does not by itself cancel it — cancel it in the Google Play subscriptions screen.
