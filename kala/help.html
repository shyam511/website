Help

Kala turns what you say into an image. Here is how to use it, and what to do when something goes wrong.

How to talk to Kala

  • Voice is always on, like a call. When you start a session the microphone stays open. You do not press anything to speak — you just talk, then pause.
  • To be silent, mute. Tap the microphone to mute; tap it again to talk. Muting ends nothing — the session is still open.
  • To change one part, draw with the pen. Tap the pen, draw over the area with your finger, and say what should change there. It is a freehand mark, not a box.
  • For a name or a date, use the keyboard. Tap the keyboard icon and type the exact words. Everything else stays voice and drawing.
  • Two directions come back. Ask for a change and you may be shown two options — say “the first”, or tap the one you want.
  • Every version stays. Open the versions list to go back to an earlier image and keep talking.

Fix a problem

  • Kala can’t hear me. The microphone may be blocked. Open Settings, tap Microphone, and allow it. You can still type instead.
  • The image failed, or a change was blocked. Your last image is always kept. Read the short reason on screen, then tap Try again — or just say “try again”.
  • You are offline. Kala needs a connection to make images. Your projects are stored on your device and are safe; reconnect and try again.
  • You have run out of free images. You have a small free allowance to try Kala. After that, a subscription keeps them coming — open Settings to subscribe or restore. You can cancel any time in Google Play.

Your images and your data

Your projects live on your device. Your prompts, images and voice are sent through our AI provider to generate results; nothing else leaves the phone. Read the privacy policy for the full detail.
